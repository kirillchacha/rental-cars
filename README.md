# Rental Cars - DizMieten Luxury Car Rental Website

A fully responsive website for DizMieten, a Swiss luxury car rental company, featuring a premium vehicle fleet, booking forms, and background video - built in German for Swiss clientele.

## 🔗 Live Demo

[https://kirillchacha.github.io/rental-cars/](https://kirillchacha.github.io/rental-cars/)

## ✨ Features

- **Hero section** - premium branding with background video showcasing the luxury fleet
- **Book a Call CTA** - prominent booking form at the top of the page
- **Vehicle fleet showcase** - 40+ high-end models (Mercedes, BMW, Audi) valued at over $50 million
- **Vehicle categories** - Premium, Business, Comfort, Sports, and Off-road selections
- **Rental options** - with or without private drivers, plus nationwide delivery across Switzerland
- **Event rentals** - dedicated sections for weddings, photo shoots, and birthdays
- **Loyalty program** - discounts up to 15% for repeat customers
- **Rapid booking** - streamlined process completed in as little as 15 minutes
- **Insurance & payment** - full insurance coverage and multiple payment methods
- **Terms & Conditions page** - legal and rental policy information
- **Responsive navigation** - header linking Home, Auto Selection, About Us, and Terms & Conditions
- **Fully responsive design** - optimized layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** - semantic markup for structure and SEO
- **CSS** - custom styling for layout, typography, and responsiveness
- **JavaScript** - interactivity and dynamic UI behavior
- **Video** - background video content for a premium visual experience
- **Custom fonts & icons** - for a polished, on-brand visual style
